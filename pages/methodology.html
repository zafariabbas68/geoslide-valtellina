Workflow

Three sequential steps

Each step feeds the next — preprocessing shapes the model, and the model drives the exposure analysis.

Step 1

Data preprocessing

Slope, aspect, plan & profile curvature derived from the DTM. Distance buffers (50 / 100 / 200 / 500 / >500 m) around roads, rivers, and faults. No-Landslide Zones defined as slope <20° or >70°, and removed wherever they overlap the IFFI inventory.

Step 2

Susceptibility model

The ModelMap R package, invoked from the QGIS Processing Toolbox, fits a generalized linear model on the sampled predictors. Sample points were generated with the QGIS Point Sampling Tool and split into training and testing sets.

Step 3

Exposure assessment

Susceptibility reclassified into four classes (low, moderate, high, very high). WorldPop raster reprojected to EPSG:32632 and combined with the classified susceptibility map using zonal statistics — producing population counts per class.

Sampling

Training & testing split

One thousand points, balanced 50 / 50 between landslide and non-landslide, split 70 / 30 between training and testing.

Field notes

Errors & how we fixed them

01

Extent mismatch

Environmental factor layers had to share identical extent and pixel size — otherwise the model crashed during calculation.

02

Filename mismatch

Layer filenames must match exactly the parameter names in the R script — otherwise the model aborted.

03

NoData collisions

NoData was set to 9999 across all layers to prevent the calculation from stopping on edge pixels.
